**Email (both apps):** nexuscoresolutions@outlook.com  

- Subject `Shifter account deletion request` or `Shifter Pro account deletion request`
- Send from your sign-in email
- Acknowledgement within 7 days; access removal within 30 days (legal retention exceptions may apply)

**Apple subscriptions (Shifter Pro):** cancel separately under **Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions**.
