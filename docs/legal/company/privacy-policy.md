## 4. Why we collect it (APP 6)

We use personal information to:

- respond to enquiries and book demos;
- provide trials and onboarding;
- provide and improve our products and website;
- send service/transactional messages;
- send marketing only where permitted (you can opt out);
- detect fraud, abuse, and security incidents;
- meet legal and accounting obligations.

## 5. Disclosure

We may disclose personal information to:

- **subprocessors** listed in our [Subprocessor List](subprocessors.md) (hosting, email, auth, payments);
- professional advisers (lawyers, accountants) under confidentiality;
- a purchaser if we sell or restructure the business (with notice where required);
- authorities where required by law.

We **do not sell** personal information.

## 7. Security (APP 11)

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the information. See our [Security Overview](security-overview.md). No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

---

## 8. Retention (APP 11.2)

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above, or as required by law (e.g. tax records). Marketing leads are reviewed periodically and deleted or de-identified when no longer needed.

## 9. Access, correction, and complaints (APPs 12–13)

You may request access to, or correction of, your personal information by contacting us. We will respond within a reasonable time.

If you have a privacy complaint, contact us first. If unresolved, you may contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC): [https://www.oaic.gov.au](https://www.oaic.gov.au).
