## 1. Scope

This DPA applies when Provider processes **Customer Personal Information** in Nexus Core, Shifter, and/or Shifter Pro on Customer’s behalf (for example participant records, progress notes, incidents, staff compliance documents, invoices generated from service delivery).

## 2. Roles

1. Customer determines the purposes and means of processing Customer Personal Information (APP entity for that information).
2. Provider processes Customer Personal Information only to provide the Services, on Customer’s documented instructions (including configuration and use of the products), and as required by law.
3. Provider acts as APP entity for Provider-owned account/billing data as described in product privacy policies.

## 3. Nature and purpose of processing

| Item | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Subject matter | Hosting and processing operational data for disability provider organisations |
| Duration | Term of the Customer agreement + wind-down/deletion period |
| Nature | Storage, transmission, display, backup, support access, sync between products |
| Purpose | Deliver scheduling, documentation, invoicing, onboarding, and related features |
| Data types | Identity, contact, NDIS/support information, health-adjacent notes, HR compliance artefacts, financial/invoice data, device logs |
| Data subjects | Participants/clients, workers, coordinators, emergency contacts, other persons Customer chooses to record |

## 4. Provider obligations

Provider will:

1. process Customer Personal Information only per this DPA and Customer instructions;
2. ensure persons authorised to process it are bound by confidentiality;
3. implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures ([Security Overview](../company/security-overview.md));
4. not engage subprocessors without offering the transparency in the Subprocessor List and the right for Customer to object to material changes on reasonable grounds;
5. assist Customer with reasonable requests related to individual access/correction where Customer cannot perform them alone through product features;
6. notify Customer without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal information breach affecting Customer Personal Information;
7. delete or return Customer Personal Information on termination at Customer’s election (subject to legal retention and backup cycles);
8. make available information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA (e.g. security overview responses), subject to confidentiality.

## 5. Customer obligations

Customer will:

1. ensure it has a lawful basis / authority to collect and upload personal information (including sensitive information where applicable);
2. configure roles and access appropriately;
3. not instruct Provider to process data unlawfully;
4. handle individual requests directed to Customer as APP entity;
5. maintain its own NDIS and privacy compliance programs.

## 6. Subprocessors

Customer authorises Provider’s use of subprocessors listed at [subprocessors.md](../company/subprocessors.md). Provider remains responsible for subprocessors’ performance under this DPA.

## 7. International transfers

Where processing involves disclosure outside Australia, Provider will take reasonable steps consistent with APP 8 (including contractual protections with subprocessors).

## 8. Audits

Upon reasonable written notice (no more than once annually, unless breach-related), Customer may request security information. On-site audits require mutual agreement, confidentiality, and reimbursement of reasonable costs unless a material breach is confirmed.

## 9. Liability

Liability under this DPA is subject to the limitations in the Customer Terms, except where prohibited by law.

## 10. Order of precedence

If there is a conflict: DPA prevails over Customer Terms for data-protection subject matter; product privacy policies explain notices to individuals but do not reduce DPA commitments.
