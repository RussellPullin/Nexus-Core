## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how we handle personal information in **Nexus Core**. Nexus Core is sold to disability provider organisations (SIL, support work, support coordination, and similar).

## 2. Roles

| Data | Who decides how it is used | Our role |
|------|----------------------------|----------|
| Customer admin accounts, billing contacts | Nexus Core Solutions | APP entity |
| Participant records, plans, case notes, staff HR docs, invoices, registers entered by the customer | Customer organisation | Customer is APP entity; we process as service provider under the [DPA](data-processing-addendum.md) |

If you are a **worker or coordinator** using Nexus Core, your organisation’s policies also apply.

## 3. Information we process

### 3.1 Account & organisation

- Name, email, role/permissions
- Organisation legal/trading name, ABN, NDIS provider number (if provided)
- Authentication data (password hashes / SSO identifiers)

### 3.2 Customer content (on behalf of the organisation)

- Participant identity and NDIS plan/budget information
- Contacts, goals, documents, case notes
- Staff profiles, compliance document expiry tracking
- Shifts, schedules, coordination tasks
- Invoices and billing summaries
- Onboarding/forms/signature artefacts
- Compliance registers and library-generated documents

### 3.3 Technical

- IP address, device/browser metadata, application logs
- Security and diagnostic events

## 4. Purposes

We use information to:

- provide, maintain, and secure Nexus Core;
- sync with linked products (Shifter / Shifter Pro) when enabled;
- send service emails/SMS where the organisation configures them;
- provide customer support;
- improve reliability and features (preferably aggregated/de-identified);
- comply with law.

We do **not** sell personal information. We do **not** use customer participant content for advertising.

## 5. Sharing

- Within the customer organisation to authorised users;
- Subprocessors in our [Subprocessor List](../company/subprocessors.md);
- At customer direction (exports, integrations such as Xero/OneDrive);
- Legal compulsion or professional advisers.

## 6. Storage & security

Data is hosted on cloud infrastructure (see Subprocessors). Controls are summarised in our [Security Overview](../company/security-overview.md).

## 7. Retention

- Account data: for the life of the subscription and a reasonable wind-down period.
- Customer content: per customer instructions and agreement; customers should export before termination.
- Logs: retained for operational/security periods then deleted or aggregated.

## 8. Your rights

Individuals may request access/correction via their organisation first (for customer content), or contact us for account data we control. Complaints: see Company Privacy Policy / OAIC.

See Subprocessors and Company Privacy Policy (APP 8).
