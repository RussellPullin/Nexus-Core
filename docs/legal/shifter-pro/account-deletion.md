**Product:** Shifter Pro (iOS / iPadOS)  
**Related privacy policy:** [Shifter Pro Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md)

> Required for Apple App Store Guideline 5.1.1(v). Publish at a stable public URL and link from App Store Connect / in-app settings.

---

## How to delete your Shifter Pro account

### Option A — Request by email (available now)

1. Email **nexuscoresolutions@outlook.com** from the email address used to sign in.
2. Subject: `Shifter Pro account deletion request`.
3. Include your full name, organisation (if any), and confirmation you want deletion.

We will acknowledge within **7 days** and delete or disable account access we control within **30 days**, except information we must retain by law or for security/dispute purposes.

### Option B — Organisation admin

Ask your Nexus Core / Shifter Pro administrator to remove your user. Email us if you need confirmation.

### Option C — In-app (when shipped)

**Settings → Account → Delete account**.

### Subscriptions purchased via Apple

Deleting your app account **does not automatically cancel** an Apple subscription. Cancel in **Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions** to avoid further Apple charges.

## What is deleted vs retained

| Data | Typical handling |
|------|------------------|
| Login / app access | Deleted or permanently disabled |
| Participant, invoice, P&L, and shift records held for an organisation | Retained for that organisation as business records |
| Apple purchase history | Controlled by Apple |
| On-device data | Remove by uninstalling / clearing app data |

Organisation-wide deletion requests: email `Organisation deletion request` (verification required).
