## 1. Introduction

Shifter Pro is an advanced mobile application for managers and field teams. In addition to shift recording and progress notes, it may include **participant and staff management**, **invoicing**, **batch billing**, **NDIS catalogue**, **P&L reporting**, and **exports**, and can sync with Nexus Core.

By using Shifter Pro, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, do not use the App.

## 2. Roles

| Information | Who is responsible |
|-------------|--------------------|
| Your login identity; Apple subscription status | We process to provide the App and fulfil subscriptions (Apple processes payments) |
| Participant/staff records, invoices, P&L, notes, incidents | Your **organisation** (or you, if you are an independent operator using the App for your business) decides purposes; we process to provide the App |

If you use Shifter Pro as a sole trader/independent support worker, you may be the APP entity for participant information you enter — you must comply with the Privacy Act and NDIS obligations that apply to you.

## 3. Information we collect

### 3.1 Account and profile

- Email, name, organisation, role
- Authentication tokens / device session data

### 3.2 Operational content (includes Shifter categories plus)

- Shift records, progress notes, incidents, travel, expenses, receipt images
- Participant / client records and assignments
- Staff management fields (as enabled)
- Invoices, batch billing artefacts, payment tracking fields
- NDIS catalogue selections / line items
- P&L and related financial summaries/exports

### 3.3 Subscription / purchase data

- Apple In-App Purchase entitlements and renewal status (payment card details are handled by Apple, not us)

### 3.4 Device, diagnostics, offline storage

Same categories as Shifter: crash/performance diagnostics; local offline database on device.

### 3.5 Permissions

Camera and Photo Library for receipts (and any other media features you enable). Deny permissions to limit those features.

## 4. How we use information

- Provide App functionality, sync, invoicing/reporting features
- Authenticate users and enforce subscription access
- Customer support and security
- Improve reliability (preferably aggregated)
- Legal compliance

We do **not** sell personal information. We do **not** use participant notes or health-adjacent content for advertising.

## 5. Sharing

- Your organisation’s authorised users (multi-tenant org mode)
- Subprocessors ([list](../company/subprocessors.md))
- Apple for purchase validation
- Customer-directed exports/sync (e.g. OneDrive, Nexus Core)
- Legal requirements

## 6. Storage and security

Cloud storage via our infrastructure providers; offline copies on device. Financial and participant data on a phone increases risk — use device passcode/biometrics and remote wipe policies. See [Security Overview](../company/security-overview.md).

## 7. Retention

- Account & entitlement data: while subscribed/active and for a reasonable period afterward
- Operational/financial records: per organisation (or your business) retention needs and law
- Apple purchase records: retained by Apple under Apple’s policies
