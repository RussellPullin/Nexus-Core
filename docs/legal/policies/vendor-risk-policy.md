## 1. Purpose

Assess and monitor vendors that process or can access Nexus Core Solutions or customer data.

## 2. When assessment is required

Before onboarding a vendor that:

- hosts or processes personal information; or  
- has administrative access to production systems; or  
- is listed (or would be listed) as a subprocessor.

## 3. Minimum due diligence

1. Identify data categories and regions  
2. Review vendor security/privacy pages and DPAs  
4. Confirm contractual confidentiality and breach notice  
5. Record decision in the evidence vault  
6. Add to [Subprocessor List](../company/subprocessors.md) if they process customer personal information for the service  

## 4. Ongoing monitoring

- Review critical vendors annually  
- Track material incidents announced by vendors  
- Remove or replace vendors that no longer meet requirements  

## 5. Customer-directed integrations

Connectors the customer enables (Xero, OneDrive, their IdP) are primarily the customer’s vendors for that data path; document the shared responsibility in the Security Overview.
