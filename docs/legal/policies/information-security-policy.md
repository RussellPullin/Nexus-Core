## 1. Purpose

Establish the principles for protecting the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of Nexus Core Solutions information assets, including customer personal information processed in Nexus Core, Shifter, and Shifter Pro.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all employees, contractors, and systems used to develop, operate, support, or market the products and company.

## 3. Policy statements

1. Information security is a management responsibility and part of day-to-day operations.  
2. Customer personal information is classified as **Confidential** (and often sensitive).  
3. Access is granted on a **least-privilege** and **need-to-know** basis.  
4. Production changes follow change management and are attributable to individuals.  
5. Security incidents are reported immediately and handled under the Data Breach Response Plan when personal information may be involved.  
6. Third parties processing data on our behalf are assessed and contracted appropriately.  
7. We do not claim external certifications we do not hold.  
8. Violations may result in access removal and, where applicable, contract or employment consequences.
