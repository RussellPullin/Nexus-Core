## 1. Introduction

Shifter is a mobile application for support workers and care staff to record shifts, progress notes, incidents, travel, and expenses, and to sync with their organisation (including Nexus Core where linked).

By using Shifter, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, do not use the App.

## 2. Roles

| Information | Who is responsible |
|-------------|--------------------|
| Your login identity (email, name, role) | Processed by us to provide the App; your organisation administers access |
| Shift notes, incidents, client assignments, receipts | Your **organisation** decides why this is collected; we process it to provide the App and sync |

Your organisation’s privacy policy and employment instructions also apply.

## 3. Information we collect

### 3.1 Account and profile

- Email address (sign-in, recovery)
- Name
- Organisation membership and role (e.g. admin, staff)

### 3.2 Work-related content

- Shift records (times, dates, clients/participants, travel, expenses)
- Progress notes (mood, activities, goals, observations)
- Incident reports and follow-up fields
- Client/participant assignments controlled by admins
- Receipt images (camera or photo library)

### 3.3 Device and technical

- Device identifiers / session tokens for authentication
- Crash and performance diagnostics (e.g. via Expo / platform tooling)
- Offline local storage of records until sync

### 3.4 Permissions

| Permission | Purpose |
|------------|---------|
| Camera | Capture receipt photos |
| Photo library | Upload existing receipt images |

You may deny permissions; receipt features will be limited.

## 4. How we use information

- Provide App functionality and sync with your organisation / Nexus Core
- Authenticate users
- Store and back up records
- Troubleshoot and improve reliability
- Comply with law

We do **not** sell personal information. We do **not** use progress notes for advertising.

## 5. Sharing

- **Your organisation** — authorised admins and users
- **Subprocessors** — see [Subprocessor List](../company/subprocessors.md) (Supabase, Azure where used, Expo/EAS, etc.)
- **Customer-directed sync** — e.g. OneDrive Excel export if enabled by your organisation
- Law enforcement or regulators when legally required

## 6. Storage and security

Data is stored in cloud infrastructure and may also be stored **offline on your device**. Use a device passcode/biometrics. See [Security Overview](../company/security-overview.md).

## 7. Retention

- Account data: while active and for a reasonable period after deletion
- Work records: per your organisation’s retention rules and legal requirements
- Local device copies: until you clear app data / uninstall (uninstall may not delete server copies held by your organisation)

## 8. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, or portability. For work content, contact your organisation first. You may also contact us at nexuscoresolutions@outlook.com.

You can request **account deletion** using the instructions at [Account Deletion — Shifter](account-deletion.md).

Complaints may be taken to the OAIC: https://www.oaic.gov.au

## 9. Children

Shifter is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect their personal information via the App.

## 10. Overseas disclosure

Some providers may process data outside Australia. See Subprocessors and the Company Privacy Policy.
