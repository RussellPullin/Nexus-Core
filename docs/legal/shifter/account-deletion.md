**Product:** Shifter (iOS / iPadOS)  
**Related privacy policy:** [Shifter Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md)

---

## How to delete your Shifter account

### Option A — Request by email (available now)

1. Send an email to **nexuscoresolutions@outlook.com** from the **same email address** used to sign in to Shifter.
2. Use the subject line: `Shifter account deletion request`.
3. Include:
   - full name;
   - organisation name (if applicable);
   - confirmation that you want the account deleted.

We will acknowledge your request within **7 days** and complete deletion of account credentials we control within **30 days**, except where we must retain information by law or for legitimate dispute/security purposes.

### Option B — Ask your organisation admin

If your login was created by an employer/provider:

1. Contact your organisation’s Nexus Core / Shifter administrator.
2. Ask them to disable or remove your user profile.
3. You may still email us if you need confirmation that platform credentials were removed.

### Option C — In-app (when shipped)

When available: **Settings → Account → Delete account** and follow the prompts.

---

## What is deleted vs retained

| Data | Typical handling |
|------|------------------|
| Login credentials / profile access | Deleted or permanently disabled |
| Shift notes, incidents, receipts already synced to your organisation | **Retained for the organisation** as their business records; not removed solely by an individual worker request |
| Local data on your phone | Clear by signing out and uninstalling the app (also delete app data in iOS Settings if needed) |

If you are an **organisation owner** requesting deletion of an entire organisation tenancy, email us with subject `Organisation deletion request` — this is handled under the customer agreement and may require verification.
