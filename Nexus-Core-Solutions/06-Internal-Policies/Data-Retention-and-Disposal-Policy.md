## 1. Purpose

Retain personal and business information only as long as needed, then dispose of it securely.

## 2. Retention schedule (defaults — customise per contract)

| Record type | Default retention | Notes |
|-------------|-------------------|-------|
| Marketing leads | 24 months from last contact | Or earlier if requested |
| Customer contracts / invoices | 7 years | Tax / corporations practice |
| Customer content in product | Term + [30–90] days wind-down | Per Customer Terms / DPA |
| Support tickets | 3 years | Reduce PII where possible |
| Security logs | [90–365] days | Longer if investigation open |
| Backups | Per provider cycle overlapping primary retention | Deletion is deferred until backup expiry |

## 3. Disposal

- Logical deletion from primary stores on schedule  
- Media sanitisation not usually applicable (cloud); rely on provider controls  
- Customer-initiated deletion requests handled under privacy policies / DPA  

## 4. Legal holds

Suspend deletion when reasonably necessary for disputes, investigations, or law.
