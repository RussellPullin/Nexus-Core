**Version:** 0.1 Draft  
**Effective date:** [INSERT DATE]  
**Owner:** [Founder / Ops]  

---

## 1. Purpose

Reduce people-related security and privacy risk for personnel who can access systems or customer data.

## 2. Pre-engagement

- Confidentiality obligations in employment/contractor agreements  
- Access only after agreement executed  

## 3. During engagement

- Security & privacy awareness on joining and annually  
- Acceptable Use acknowledgement  
- No unauthorised copying of customer participant data to personal devices/accounts  

## 4. Support access ethics

Access customer environments only for legitimate support, security, or operations; prefer screenshare/customer-driven actions when feasible; log significant production access.

## 5. Exit

Follow Access Control Policy leaver process within 24 hours: revoke SaaS admin, cloud, source control, email, device accounts; recover company devices.
