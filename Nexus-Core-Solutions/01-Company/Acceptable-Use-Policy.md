## 1. Purpose

This AUP sets rules for using our products so that platforms remain secure, lawful, and suitable for disability provider operations.

## 2. Prohibited conduct

You must not:

1. Use the services for unlawful purposes, including fraud, harassment, or discrimination.
2. Upload or process content you are not authorised to handle (including participant data without a lawful basis / organisational authority).
3. Attempt unauthorised access to accounts, systems, or data of other organisations.
4. Probe, scan, or load-test our systems without prior written approval.
5. Introduce malware, or interfere with service integrity or availability.
6. Resell, white-label, or sublicense the services except as expressly allowed in your customer agreement.
7. Misrepresent your identity, organisation, or NDIS registration status.
8. Use the services to send spam or unsolicited bulk communications unrelated to legitimate service delivery.
9. Bypass usage limits, security controls, or billing mechanisms.
10. Use outputs or exports to build a competing product in violation of your agreement.

## 3. Customer responsibilities

Customer organisations must:

- configure access (roles, invites) appropriately;
- ensure workers are trained on privacy and incident recording expectations;
- maintain their own NDIS and workplace compliance obligations;
- promptly revoke access when staff leave.

## 4. Enforcement

We may investigate suspected violations, suspend or terminate access, remove content, and report unlawful activity to authorities or affected organisations, consistent with our customer terms and law.

## 5. Reporting

Report abuse or security concerns to: nexuscoresolutions@outlook.com
